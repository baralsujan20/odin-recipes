Chowmin

Description

Chow mein is a noodle dish made by stir-frying noodles with vegetables, meat, and flavorful sauces.

It is a quick and tasty meal that can be prepared with different vegetables and proteins.

Ingredients

  • Noodles
  • Chicken
  • Carrot
  • Cabbage
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Soy sauce
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Boil the noodles and drain them.
  2. Cut the vegetables and chicken into small pieces.
  3. Heat oil in a pan.
  4. Cook the chicken until done.
  5. Add the vegetables and stir-fry them.
  6. Add the cooked noodles.
  7. Add soy sauce, salt, and black pepper.
  8. Stir-fry everything together.
  9. Serve hot.
