Momo

Description

Momo is a popular South Asian dumpling commonly filled with vegetables or meat and served with a spicy dipping sauce.

The dumplings are usually steamed and are popular as a snack or meal.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Ground chicken
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Cabbage
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Water

Steps

  1. Prepare the dough using flour and water.
  2. Mix the chicken with onion, garlic, ginger, and seasoning.
  3. Roll the dough into small thin circles.
  4. Place the filling in the center of each wrapper.
  5. Fold and seal the momo.
  6. Place the momo in a steamer.
  7. Steam until fully cooked.
  8. Serve hot with dipping sauce.
