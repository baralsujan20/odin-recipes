Lasanga

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta,meat sauce, cheese, and tomato sauce.

The layers are baked together until the cheese becomes golden and the flavors combine.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna pasta
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Cook the lasagna pasta according to the instructions.
  2. Cook the ground beef with onion and garlic.
  3. Add the tomato sauce and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Layer the pasta, meat sauce, and cheese in a baking dish.
  5. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  6. Bake until the cheese is melted and golden.
  7. Allow the lasagna to cool slightly before serving.
